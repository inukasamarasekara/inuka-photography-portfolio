01 / The person behind the camera

Not just taking pictures. Keeping stories.

I'm Inuka Samarasekara, a photographer from Sri Lanka with a passion for capturing people, atmosphere and meaningful moments. My work is centred on portrait and event photography — from personal celebrations to formal occasions.

I believe a good photograph should do more than look beautiful. It should bring you back to the feeling of being there. I aim to keep every frame natural, thoughtful and full of character.
